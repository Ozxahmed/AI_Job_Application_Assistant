# Cover Letter Info

## Core Background Summary

Write a short reusable summary of your background, strongest skills, domain experience, and the kind of work you want to do.

## Reusable Short Cover Statement

I am interested in this role because my background combines {core_skills} with experience building {systems_or_outputs}. I am looking to apply that experience in a role focused on {role_focus}.

## Strongest Themes To Reuse

- Theme 1: technical strength, domain knowledge, or measurable impact.
- Theme 2: another recurring strength.
- Theme 3: a role-specific angle.

## Role-Specific Adaptation Notes

For data engineering roles, emphasize pipeline reliability, data modeling, cloud systems, orchestration, and data quality.

For analytics engineering roles, emphasize SQL models, stakeholder-facing datasets, dashboards, and metric clarity.

For domain-specific roles, emphasize relevant business context and the ability to translate business needs into data assets.
